## 7. Route Options

### Option A: Warm Path Through Ken

Best when:

- Human approves using the relationship.
- The sender has a genuine connection to Ken.
- The goal is to ask whether Ken is the right person or who should own the conversation.

Pros:

- Senior operator/co-owner path.
- Less cold.
- Can ask routing without assuming buyer identity.

Cons:

- If Ken is not the right buyer, message needs careful routing language.
- Need to avoid implying food savings or overspending.

Recommendation: use this route for beta if relationship approval is granted.

### Option B: Direct Company / Finance Outreach

Best when:

- Relationship path is not approved.
- Finance contact can be verified.

Pros:

- Cleaner buyer alignment if controller/CFO is identified.

Cons:

- Finance contact is less verified at this stage.
- Cold finance outreach may be weaker than a warm owner/operator route.

Recommendation: hold until finance contact is verified.

## 8. Recommended Route

Recommended first route: Option A, warm path through Ken Martin.

Reason:

- Ken appears to be a senior operator/co-owner and is likely close enough to the decision to either engage or route appropriately.
- This tests the beta workflow for near-buyer relationship paths.

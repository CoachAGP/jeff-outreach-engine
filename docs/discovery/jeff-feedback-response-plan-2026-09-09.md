# Jeff Feedback Response Plan - September 9, 2026

Status: beta workflow correction plan.

Purpose: convert Jeff's week-one feedback into concrete product changes before the next live beta run.

## Product Direction

The agent can research, score, and draft, but the chat-first delivery is not acceptable for Jeff's daily use. The next version will behave like an approval queue: messages first, decisions second, supporting research linked below.

## Required Changes

1. Conserve credits by assigning a preliminary fit score before deep research.
2. Present draft messages as the primary review item.
3. Keep full research available through links instead of mixing it into the draft.
4. Require each agent run to complete its checklist or state the exact blocker.
5. Preserve human approval before every outgoing communication.

## Tuesday ZoomInfo Run

The Tuesday run should initially produce only:

- Company and intent data from the ZoomInfo report.
- Initial fit score and short reason.
- Research, hold, reject, or clarification status.
- Top five research candidates.
- Top three deep-report candidates.

Deep research and drafting begin only for the selected opportunities.

## Credit-Control Research Gate

- Use intake data and lightweight checks to assign a preliminary 1-5 fit score, with 5 as the best fit.
- Scores 1-2 do not receive deep research. Mark them hold, reject, or needs clarification and provide a short reason.
- Scores 3-5 qualify for deep research, processed in the order 5, 4, then 3.
- After deep research, confirm or revise the score before recommending outreach.

## Dashboard Requirements

Each opportunity must show the company, fit score, route, contact, draft, QA status, decision needed, next action, and full report link. Jeff must be able to mark an item approved for manual send, needing edits, on hold, or rejected.

## Success Test

Jeff should be able to review the queue in under five minutes and answer: what is ready, what needs approval, what is blocked, what must be checked in HubSpot or Athena, and which companies are next.

### Reasonable Indicators

- Fleet, logistics, inventory, purchasing, and multi-branch operations suggest relevant indirect-spend categories.
- Current hiring for operations and purchasing roles suggests ongoing operational growth or staffing needs.
- Public revenue estimates indicate likely middle-market fit.

### Hypotheses

- NCBP may have addressable opportunities in logistics, fleet, route planning, facilities, insurance, telecom, technology, purchasing, and branch-level operating spend.
- Multi-location operations may create duplication or fragmented purchasing.
- A complimentary spend assessment may be valuable, but this cannot be claimed without direct conversation and data review.

## 7. Route Options

### Option A: Warm Path Through Maria

Best when:

- Human approves using the relationship.
- The goal is a respectful internal routing request.
- Sender has a real relationship with Maria.

Pros:

- Less cold.
- Helps avoid guessing the right internal route.
- Respects company hierarchy.

Cons:

- Maria may not be the right person.
- Could create awkwardness if the relationship is too personal or weak.

Recommendation: use if the relationship is genuine and approved.

### Option B: Direct CFO Outreach to John Hutt

Best when:

- Human does not want to use Maria.

- Directly aligned with spend optimization.

Cons:

Recommendation: acceptable if warm path is not approved.

# Beta 3-Company Dry Run

Status: beta dry run v0.1.

Purpose: test the Prospect Fit Scoring and Compliance / QA Check skills against three real candidate companies. This document is research and draft preparation only. It does not approve outreach.

Companies tested:

1. Colony Grill
2. New Castle Building Products
3. TVG Fast and Fresh / Jimmy John's Connecticut

## Executive Summary

| Company | Fit Score | Decision | Contact Path | Beta Action |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Colony Grill | 5/5 | Excellent fit | Ken Martin appears senior enough for warm path; finance structure still needs verification | Prepare human-review outreach draft after confirming route. |
| New Castle Building Products | 5/5 | Excellent fit | Maria Kotereva is a finance-team path; CFO John Hutt is the likely direct buyer | Route via Maria only if human-approved; otherwise CFO is target. |
| TVG Fast and Fresh / Jimmy John's CT | 4/5 | Strong fit | Will Roth appears owner/franchisee path | Prepare draft focused on franchise-operating categories, not food savings first. |

Recommended first live beta company: New Castle Building Products.

Why: strongest spend fit, visible CFO, multi-location/distribution profile, and Maria creates a warm finance path without needing to guess the buyer.

## 1. Colony Grill

Prospect Fit Score: 5/5

Decision: Excellent fit.

### Top Reasons

- Multi-location restaurant group.
- Restaurant groups are a confirmed beta-priority segment.
- Ken Martin appears publicly as a senior operator/co-owner figure.
- Likely relevant categories include food, waste, facilities, janitorial, uniforms/linens, insurance, merchant services, technology, and telecom.
- Current/recent public activity around Colony Grill's brand and leadership provides topical context.

### Caution Flags

- CFO/controller/finance owner is not yet verified.
- Restaurant food sourcing may be category-sensitive, especially if the brand has specific vendor/product requirements.
- Do not lead with a claim that savings exist.

### Fact vs Inference

- Verified fact: Public sources identify Ken Martin with Colony Grill and senior leadership/operator language.
- Verified fact: Public company/profile sources identify Colony Grill as a restaurant group.
- Reasonable indicator: Multi-location restaurants may have relevant spend in waste, facilities, janitorial, insurance, merchant services, uniforms/linens, and technology.
- Hypothesis: Food-cost opportunity may exist, but it should not be claimed before discovery.

### Contact Assessment

Known contact: Ken Martin.

Assessment: likely senior operator / owner path. He may be a valid first conversation path, but the ideal buyer should still be verified as CFO, controller, owner, or finance sponsor.

Confidence: medium-high.

### Recommended Next Action

Build a deeper company brief and identify CFO/controller/finance owner. If the relationship path is approved, use Ken as warm entry rather than cold outreach.

### Human-Review Draft Angle

Focus on multi-location restaurant operating spend and third-party validation. Avoid "you are overspending" and avoid promising food-cost savings.

### Draft Opening Message

Subject: Quick question on Colony Grill operating spend

Ken,

I noticed Colony Grill's continued growth and multi-location footprint. I work with middle-market operators to review indirect operating spend across areas like facilities, waste, insurance, merchant services, technology, uniforms, and related vendor categories.

The starting point is a no-obligation conversation about whether ERA Group's indirect spend expertise may be relevant. No assumptions that savings exist, just a structured way to see whether there are categories worth validating.

Would it be worth a short conversation?

QA result: PASS WITH HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED.

Required human edits before use:

- Confirm sender relationship to Ken.
- Confirm whether this should reference the warm relationship or remain cold.
- Confirm whether to include a specific asset.

## 2. New Castle Building Products

Prospect Fit Score: 5/5

Decision: Excellent fit.

### Top Reasons

- Building products distributor profile likely includes meaningful indirect spend.
- Public sources show multiple locations and operating departments such as purchasing, inventory, logistics, fleet, IT, finance, and credit.
- Public sources identify CFO John E. Hutt.
- Public source identifies Maria Kotereva in the finance department, making her a possible warm internal path.
- Estimated public revenue and employee data indicate middle-market fit.

### Caution Flags

- Revenue estimates from enrichment sources should be treated as indicators, not verified financial facts.
- Maria is not the final buyer based on public role signal; she is a path to finance.
- Need to verify whether HubSpot/Athena already has this account or an active owner.

### Fact vs Inference

- Verified fact: New Castle Building Products' website lists John E. Hutt as Chief Financial Officer.
- Verified fact: New Castle's team page lists Maria Kotereva in the finance department.
- Verified fact: Public company materials describe operational growth and locations.
- Reasonable indicator: Building products distribution suggests freight, logistics, fleet, facilities, insurance, IT, and purchasing/inventory spend.
- Hypothesis: There may be cost optimization opportunities, but this requires assessment and cannot be claimed.

### Contact Assessment

Known contact: Maria Kotereva.

Assessment: finance-team warm path. Best direct buyer appears to be CFO John E. Hutt, while Maria could help route or validate the right finance owner if human-approved.

Confidence: high that Maria is a useful path; high that CFO is the ideal target based on public source.

### Recommended Next Action

Check HubSpot/Athena for existing record, owner, client status, or active opportunity. If clear, prepare a human-reviewed warm-intro request to Maria or direct CFO outreach for human approval.

### Human-Review Draft Angle

Focus on distribution operating spend, logistics/fleet/facilities/purchasing categories, and no-obligation validation.

### Draft Opening Message

Subject: New Castle Building Products spend review question

John,

New Castle's distribution footprint looks like the kind of operation where indirect spend can sit across several departments: logistics, fleet, facilities, technology, insurance, purchasing, and branch-level operating categories.

I work with ERA Group to help middle-market organizations reduce indirect supplier spend with deep category expertise, no upfront cost, and no additional cost to the business. The spend assessment is one tool we use to validate whether there is a practical opportunity before anyone commits time to a project.

Would a short introductory conversation be reasonable?

QA result: PASS WITH HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED.

Required human edits before use:

- Confirm whether outreach should go through Maria first.
- Confirm whether the sender has permission to use Maria as a relationship path.
- Confirm HubSpot/Athena status before drafting final outreach.

## 3. TVG Fast and Fresh / Jimmy John's Connecticut

Prospect Fit Score: 4/5

Decision: Strong fit.

### Top Reasons

- Multi-location restaurant/franchise operator.
- Public sources identify William Roth / Will Roth as owner or co-owner tied to Connecticut Jimmy John's locations.
- Public articles identify Norwalk, Orange, and East Haven Connecticut locations.
- Restaurant groups are in beta priority, and the lower-revenue restaurant exception applies when operating spend is meaningful.
- Newer location openings create a current operating/growth trigger.

### Caution Flags

- Franchise purchasing constraints may limit food-cost flexibility.
- Organization may be below normal revenue floor, so category spend must be validated.
- Need to verify exact ownership structure and whether TVG Fast and Fresh is the correct operating entity for all locations.
- Do not lead with food-cost savings unless approved and contextually relevant.

### Fact vs Inference

- Verified fact: Public reporting identifies William Roth as co-owner of TVG Fast and Fresh, LLC, a Jimmy John's franchisee.
- Verified fact: Public reporting describes Connecticut Jimmy John's locations in Norwalk, Orange, and East Haven.
- Reasonable indicator: Multi-location franchise restaurants may have relevant spend in facilities, janitorial, uniforms, waste, merchant services, insurance, technology, and possibly food where allowed.
- Hypothesis: Franchise constraints may limit supplier flexibility; this should be discovered, not assumed.

### Contact Assessment

Known contact: Will Roth.

Assessment: likely owner/franchisee path and may be a valid first conversation target. If not final decision-maker, he is likely close to the decision.

Confidence: high for relationship path, medium-high for buyer path.

### Recommended Next Action

Prepare a human-review draft to Will focused on non-food operating categories first, with a discovery question about franchise purchasing constraints.

### Human-Review Draft Angle

Focus on multi-location restaurant operating categories where franchisees may retain flexibility.

### Draft Opening Message

Subject: Quick operating-cost question for your Jimmy John's group

Will,

I saw the continued Connecticut expansion for your Jimmy John's group. I work with operators to review indirect spend categories where multi-location restaurants may still have flexibility, such as waste, facilities, janitorial, insurance, merchant services, uniforms, technology, and related operating expenses.

The first step is only to see whether ERA Group's indirect spend expertise may be relevant. No upfront cost, no obligation, and no assumption that savings exist before the data is reviewed.

Would it be worth a short conversation?

QA result: PASS WITH HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED.

Required human edits before use:

- Confirm relationship context.
- Confirm whether franchise food purchasing should be avoided in the first message.
- Confirm no active HubSpot/Athena record or owner conflict.

## Compliance / QA Summary

All three drafts:

- Avoid promising savings.
- Avoid saying the prospect is overspending.
- Avoid claiming savings have already been found.
- Avoid asking for AP data in first-touch outreach.
- Require human approval before sending.
- Use no-obligation / no-upfront-cost language in the approved safe context.

Potential issues before live use:

- Relationship-path usage must be explicitly approved.
- HubSpot/Athena status must be checked.
- Any mutual relationship reference must be accurate and human-approved.
- Direct-mail or phone follow-up is out of scope for this dry run unless separately approved.

## Recommended Next Step

Use New Castle Building Products as the first live beta test because:

- It has the strongest visible finance path.
- It has clear relevant spend categories.
- It is less constrained than restaurant franchise targets.
- It tests the Maria warm-path workflow without forcing Maria to be the final buyer.

Before any outreach:

1. Check HubSpot and Athena for existing account/client/opportunity/owner.
2. Confirm whether Maria may be used as a warm path.
3. Confirm whether the sender wants a warm-intro request draft or a direct CFO draft.
4. Run final Compliance / QA Check on the chosen final message.

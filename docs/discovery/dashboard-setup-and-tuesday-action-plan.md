## Current beta behavior

1. Paste company names from the Tuesday ZoomInfo emails, one name per line, into New companies. Duplicates are skipped by company name.
2. Assign a preliminary fit score from 1 to 5 using only the available intake evidence. Leave unknown companies unscored. ZoomInfo indicators are not independently verified facts.
3. Scores 3, 4, and 5 automatically appear in the copyable research request, in descending score order. Scores 1 and 2 stop at triage. The dashboard does not launch research or consume credits itself.
4. Once research is complete, the company review should present a short summary, GitHub report link, and prefilled draft together in one row. Jeff can edit the draft there.
5. Mark conflict Yes or No, check QA passed, and check HubSpot entered and reviewed. A Yes conflict or any unchecked gate blocks local approval.
6. Jeff marks the draft approved only after those checks. This is a local record of review, not an authenticated signature or an email send. Jeff sends manually after final review.

The current static dashboard includes three prepared beta examples. New intake companies do not automatically become researched review rows: Codex must complete the research and publish a report/draft first. The page has no direct HubSpot write connection.

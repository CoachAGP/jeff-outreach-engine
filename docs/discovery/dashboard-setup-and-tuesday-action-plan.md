## Current beta behavior

1. Paste company names from the Tuesday ZoomInfo emails, one name per line, into Tuesday Intake. Duplicates are skipped by company name.
2. Assign a preliminary fit score from 1 to 5 using only the available intake evidence. Leave unknown companies unscored. ZoomInfo and Athena indicators are not independently verified facts.
3. Mark the Athena check. A known conflict disables research queueing. Resolve unknowns before outreach.
4. Queue only scores 3, 4, and 5 for research; work in descending score order. Scores 1 and 2 stop at triage.
5. The dashboard's queue is local to the browser/device. It does not launch Codex research, consume ZoomInfo credits, or write HubSpot records.
6. After research and a route decision, place a QA-checked message in the approval queue. Jeff approves the final wording before he sends anything.

## Today: Tuesday ZoomInfo intake

## 4. Fact vs Inference

### Verified Facts

- Public reporting identifies William Roth as co-owner of TVG Fast and Fresh, LLC.
- Public reporting identifies TVG Fast and Fresh as a Jimmy John's franchisee.
- Public reporting ties the group to Connecticut locations in Norwalk, Orange, and East Haven.
- Public reporting identifies the East Haven restaurant as the group's third Connecticut location.

### Reasonable Indicators

- Multi-location quick-service restaurant operations suggest spend in waste, facilities, janitorial, repairs/maintenance, insurance, merchant services, uniforms, technology, telecom, and local operating supplies.
- Franchise restaurants may have limited flexibility on required food purchasing but more flexibility in local operating categories.
- New-location growth may create interest in validating recurring operating costs.

### Hypotheses

- TVG Fast and Fresh may have addressable spend in non-food operating categories.
- Food-cost opportunity may be limited by franchise requirements.
- A complimentary spend assessment may be relevant if enough category spend exists.

## 5. Likely Relevant Spend Categories

Lead with non-food categories:

- Waste management
- Facilities management
- Repairs and maintenance
- Janitorial / cleaning
- Insurance
- Merchant card services
- Technology / POS / telecom
- Uniforms
- Local operating supplies
- Payroll / HR administration, depending on structure

Use caution with:

- Food purchasing
- Beverage/supply purchasing
- Franchise-controlled categories

Do not claim TVG Fast and Fresh or Jimmy John's is overspending in any category.
